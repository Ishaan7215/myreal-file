India

  • Commissioned by: Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1631 in memory of his favorite wife, Mumtaz Mahal.
  • Timeline: Construction began in 1632 and finished around 1653.
  • Main Architect: Ustad Ahmad Lahori.
  • Design & Style: A masterwork of Mughal architecture combining Islamic, Persian, Ottoman, and Indian styles.
  • Design Features: It features a large central white dome, four corner minarets tilted slightly outward for safety during earthquakes, delicate pietra dura inlay work with semi-precious stones, and a formal Charbagh (four-part) garden.
  • Status: Designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983 and recognized as one of the wonders of the world.
